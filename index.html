Patient Education & Guidance

Dental & Aesthetic Myths Busted

Evidence-based insights from Dr. Anisha Singh on gum health, root coverage, teeth cleaning, and medical skin rejuvenation.

Common Myth

"Receding gums and exposed roots can never be restored."

Clinical Fact:

False! Using advanced Perio Plastic Surgery and the Single Incision CTG Technique for Root Coverage along with Injectable PRF, receding gum lines can be predictably regenerated with minimal discomfort.

Common Myth

"Teeth cleaning (scaling) weakens teeth or damages enamel."

Clinical Fact:

Completely False! Professional ultrasonic teeth cleaning & polishing only removes plaque, tartar, and bacterial calculus. Leaving tartar untreated causes swollen, bleeding gums and bone loss.

Common Myth

"Salon facials give the same results as clinical Medifacials."

Clinical Fact:

Not at all! Clinical treatments like the Radiance Signature HydraFacial, Chemical Peels, Microneedling, and PRP work at deeper dermal layers under a certified cosmetologist's sterile supervision for real cellular renewal.

Varanasi Oral & Aesthetic Advisory

When Should You Visit Radiance Oral Care Immediately?

Do not ignore bleeding or swollen gums, root sensitivity from receding gums, missing teeth, dark gums, persistent toothache, or dull/aging skin concerns. Early intervention ensures painless, minimally invasive recovery!
